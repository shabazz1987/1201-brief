Morning talk-back

Secretary chat opens with the 12:01 Daily link. Then one or two questions. Not a wall of the brief.

Live alerts

New inbound, calendar (flight in four hours), people waiting. No Daily link on these. A draft sits in the alert. Hassan’s reply in chat is the send.

Meeting prep

About 30 minutes before a real meeting. Who, last notes, what he owes. Skip holds, focus blocks, travel buffers. Travel stays a flight alert.

What it is not

Not a chat wall. Not Salesforce. Not Gong. Not email sent on its own. Lindsee owns studio mail as herself. Secretary briefs Hassan.
