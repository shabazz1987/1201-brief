12:01 Daily

A morning page for Hassan Rahim, creative director of 1201. What needs him today.

Every day at 7am PT, a hosted page tells him what needs him. A software secretary reads his live tools and writes that page. Chat is talk-back after. Nothing is invented. Nothing is sent unless he says yes.
