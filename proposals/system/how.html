Sources

Superhuman is all mail: hr@1201.am and h@hassanrahim.com. Gmail is only a fallback if Superhuman cannot see an inbox. Google Calendar is the source of truth for work and personal. Label ET vs PT. Skip junk holds. Discord is Midnight Oil. Fathom / Granola for meetings. ClickUp, due ±14 days or updated this week. Dropbox tap links. Vault: who people are, what he already promised.

After the page

Talk-back is short chat after the page. Live alerts: someone waiting, a draft sits ready, his reply is the send. Meeting prep is 30 minutes before a real meeting — who, last notes, what he owes.
